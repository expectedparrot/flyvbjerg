# Draft reference-class definition

## Unit

One newly awarded MLB expansion franchise, beginning with formal league award
and continuing through launch and later competitive milestones.

## Initial class

The four most recent MLB expansion franchises: Colorado Rockies, Florida
Marlins, Arizona Diamondbacks, and Tampa Bay Devil Rays.

## Exclusions

- Franchise relocations, including the Seattle Pilots becoming the Milwaukee
  Brewers after the Pilots' inaugural 1969 season.
- Earlier expansion waves for this first pass; league economics, media,
  divisional structure, and venue context may be less comparable.
- Minor-league and non-MLB franchise launches.

## Launch is not one date

The persistent franchise case has a lifecycle including league award, naming,
venue agreement, executive staffing, player acquisition, ticket sales, spring
training, inaugural game, and later milestones. Analyses select the event pair
that defines their duration.

## Cohort dependence

The teams launched in paired league expansion programs. Members of a pair share
league rules, expansion-draft design, timing, and macro environment. They are
not four fully independent observations.
