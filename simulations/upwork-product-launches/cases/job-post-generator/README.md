# Job Post Generator

Upwork's July 2023 announcement described a beta feature that creates editable
job-post drafts for clients. By April 2024, Upwork described it as powered or
improved by Uma.

Later company-reported evidence is unusually concrete: FY2023 results said
clients using it could post jobs almost 70% faster; Q3 2024 results reported 73%
faster completion than job posts in that quarter not using the feature.

This looks measurable, but the comparison is observational and disclosed by the
company. It establishes a workflow-speed claim, not causal business impact or
overall product success.
