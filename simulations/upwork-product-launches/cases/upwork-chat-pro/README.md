# Upwork Chat Pro

Premiered in November 2023 as a GPT-4-powered beta with a waitlist and planned
select-user rollout. By Q1 2024 it was bundled into Freelancer Plus, and Upwork
reported strong adoption. In Q2 2024, Upwork reported daily active users rose
68% quarter over quarter.

The dossier exposes a launch-date problem: announcement, selected-user access,
subscription bundling, and broad availability are different transitions.
