# Friction log

- “Profitability” cannot be inferred exactly from production budget and gross.
  The package needs first-class proxy labels and threshold sensitivity.
- Budget ranges must remain ranges; midpoint substitution creates false
  precision and can change threshold classification.
- 2021 releases combine theatrical and streaming strategies and pandemic-era
  constraints. They remain eligible by the predeclared release rule but should
  be shown as a contextual cluster.
- Nominal dollars are sufficient for within-film ratios, but budget and gross
  levels across years would require inflation adjustment.
- A famous-film seed cohort is not demonstrably exhaustive even when every
  included film has complete financial fields.
