# Friction log

- Wikipedia usually supports announcement and completion chronology, but legal
  completion and first trading sometimes differ by one or two days.
- Multi-stage breakups need both first and full completion endpoints.
- Plan revisions require a first-class event or claim; overwriting the original
  plan would introduce hindsight.
- An eight-case hand-built cohort is auditable but not demonstrably exhaustive.
  Its proportions should be labeled provisional.
- The current dependence control is analysis-level clustering. This dataset
  avoids false precision by making the parent plan, rather than each successor,
  the unit of analysis.
- A zero-day phasing lag is substantively valid when first and full completion
  are the same supported event date. The metric must distinguish that valid
  zero from an absent or malformed endpoint.
