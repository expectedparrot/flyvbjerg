# Upwork pricing and monetization — clean intake-first simulation

This is a second, independent pre-implementation simulation of Flyvbjerg. It
uses Upwork earnings materials to explore pricing changes and monetization
features. It does not inherit cases or assumptions from the product-launch
simulation.

## Research question

What pricing changes and monetization mechanisms did Upwork introduce or
materially change from 2023 through 2024, and what later evidence did Upwork
disclose about their effects or commercial performance?

## Scope of this pass

- Primary materials only: Upwork shareholder letters, prepared remarks,
  financial-results releases, investor presentations, and Form 10-K.
- Source pages are registered by URL and retrieval date. No local captures were
  downloaded in this simulation.
- Disclosures are preserved first as intake claims. They are not automatically
  attributed to product cases or treated as comparable observations.
- The collection is exploratory, not an approved reference class.

## Candidate monetization cases

| case | mechanism | earliest evidence in this pass |
|---|---|---:|
| Flat 10% freelancer service fee | transaction fee pricing change | 2023-05-03 |
| Contract initiation fee | client fixed fee on new contracts | April 2023 |
| Connects monetization | paid virtual tokens / bidding and ads | established; material 2023 changes |
| Freelancer Plus pricing and packaging | talent subscription | material Q2 2024 change |
| Availability Badge | paid availability signal | active during 2023 |
| Boosted Proposals | paid proposal placement | active during 2023 |

## What the evidence can support

- Dates and descriptions of several pricing interventions.
- Company-reported portfolio and component revenue growth.
- Marketplace and total take-rate movements.
- Product-specific marketplace outcome claims for Availability Badge and
  Boosted Proposals.
- Explicit management attribution in some periods.

## What it cannot yet support

- A causal estimate for each individual intervention.
- A common success metric across transaction fees, tokens, subscriptions, and
  ads products.
- Allocation of aggregate Ads & Monetization revenue growth among its component
  products.
- Customer-welfare or marketplace-health effects from revenue growth alone.

See `intake/items.jsonl` for the raw claim layer and `friction-log.md` for the
modeling lessons.
