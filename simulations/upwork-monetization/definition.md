# Exploratory definition

## Candidate unit

One distinct customer-facing pricing rule, paid marketplace mechanism, or
subscription packaging intervention operated by Upwork.

## Provisional inclusion

- Changes what a client or freelancer pays, receives in a paid package, or can
  purchase to affect marketplace visibility or participation.
- Identifiable as a persistent mechanism or a versioned pricing intervention.
- Supported by an Upwork primary source.

## Provisional exclusions

- Enterprise sales-process changes without a customer-facing pricing or product
  mechanism.
- General marketplace improvements that affect revenue but are not monetization
  mechanisms.
- Aggregate financial metrics themselves; those are claims or observations, not
  product cases.

## Unresolved unit problem

A persistent product such as Freelancer Plus can undergo several packaging and
price changes. The case should be the persistent offering; each material pricing
or packaging change should be an event. By contrast, replacing the tiered
freelancer service fee with a flat fee may itself be best modeled as a versioned
pricing-policy case. Simulation should test this asymmetry rather than conceal
it.
